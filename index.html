FAQ

How do I cancel my auto-renewing subscription?

On your iPhone/iPad, go to Settings, tap your Apple ID, then choose Subscriptions. Select FocusBill and tap Cancel Subscription. Please note that if you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the subscription will automatically renew.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. In the app, tap “Restore Purchase” while signed in with the same Apple ID, and your membership benefits will be restored.

How is my data saved?

FocusBill saves all your records and coin data both locally on your device and in iCloud. This dual backup helps keep your data safe from loss.

Can I sync my data across devices?

Yes. Your data can be automatically synced across devices signed into the same iCloud account.

Will my data be lost if I delete the app?

No. Your data remains safely stored in iCloud. When you reinstall FocusBill and sign in with the same Apple ID, the app will automatically restore your data from the cloud. Your records will not disappear unless you manually delete them from iCloud.

Can I export Focus Bills?

Yes. On the Records screen, tap the Download button to generate an image of your bill and save it to your photo library. When asked for permission on the first export, choose “Allow Adding Photos” to save normally.

常见问题

如何取消自动续费？

在 iPhone/iPad 上，前往「设置」> 点击顶部您的 Apple ID > 「订阅」，选择 FocusBill 后点击取消订阅。请注意，当前订阅周期到期前 24 小时未取消将自动续费。

购买会员后能否恢复？

可以。在 App 内点击「恢复购买」，使用同一 Apple ID 即可恢复已购买的会员权益。

数据怎样保存？

FocusBill 会将你的所有记录与金币数据保存在 App 本地及 iCloud 云端，双重保障，数据不易丢失。

数据可以跨设备同步吗？

可以，数据可以在登录同一 iCloud 账户的设备间自动同步。

卸载 App 后数据会丢失吗？

不会丢失。卸载后数据仍安全保存在 iCloud 中。再次下载并登录同一 Apple ID 时，App 会自动从云端恢复数据；除非在 iCloud 中手动删除，否则记录不会消失。

专注账单支持导出吗？

支持。您可以在「记录」页面点击「下载」按钮，将账单生成图片并保存到系统相册。首次导出时需授权 FocusBill 访问相册，请选择「允许添加照片」即可正常保存。
